HOME MONTAGE VIDEO
==================
To add the homepage background video, save your file here as:

    home-montage.mp4

Recommended specs:
  - MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
  - 1920x1080 (16:9) recommended
  - Muted (audio is not played; autolay requires muted)
  - Compressed for web (10-30MB is ideal)
  - Loop-friendly (no obvious start/end jump)

Once home-montage.mp4 is placed in this folder, refresh the homepage
and the video will autoplay as the hero background.  The silver gradient
is the fallback shown until the video is added.

You can also save a JPEG still frame here as:

    video-poster.jpg  (in /public/uploads/images/home/)

This image is shown before the video starts playing.
